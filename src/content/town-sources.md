# Town fact sources

Facts on the town pages come from these pages. Anything that was not on one of them was left out. Inspector names and personal phone numbers from the state list are not repeated on the site.

## Brandon

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon,_South_Dakota — Minnehaha County, five miles east of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 11,048, incorporated 1973, ZIP 57005, Brandon Valley School District.
- https://cityofbrandon.org/inspections — Planning and Inspection Department, 304 Main Ave., Brandon, SD 57005.

## Harrisburg

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrisburg,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, suburb of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 6,732, founded 1873, ZIP 57032, Harrisburg School District, several schools inside Sioux Falls because district and city lines do not match.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_South_Dakota — county seat is Canton; Harrisburg School District is one of the county districts.

## Tea

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, suburb of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 5,598, founded 1894, ZIP 57064, Tea Area School District.
- https://www.teaarea.k12.sd.us/page/contact-us — Legacy, Venture, middle school, and high school addresses in Tea; Frontier Elementary is in Sioux Falls.
- https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/results.aspx?districtnumber=41005 — Tea Area 41-5, 131 N Poplar, Tea.

Left out: driving miles and Highway 42. Those showed up on a real-estate page, not on a city, county, or Wikipedia page.

## Hartford

- https://www.hartfordsd.us/welcome — city site: about 10 miles west of Sioux Falls; West Central schools.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_South_Dakota — Minnehaha County, suburban city northwest of Sioux Falls, West Central High School, 2020 population 3,354, district also covers Humboldt, the Wall Lake area, and Ellis.

## Dell Rapids

- https://dot.sd.gov/media/079ac1ff/DellRapids_MTP_Final.pdf — northern Minnehaha County, about 15 miles north of Sioux Falls on the I-29 corridor, Big Sioux River, downtown about three miles from I-29 via SD Highway 115, Old Highway 77 north of the city. The plan’s 2017 population figure is not used as a current count.
- https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/results.aspx?districtnumber=49003 — Dell Rapids School District 49-3, 1216 N Garfield Ave.

Left out: a current census population. It was not in the sources pulled for this pass.

## Canton

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton,_South_Dakota — county seat of Lincoln County, 18 miles south of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 3,066, incorporated 1881, ZIP 57013.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_South_Dakota — Canton School District 41-1 is a district in the county.

## Lennox

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lennox,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, 20 miles southwest of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 2,423, founded 1889, ZIP 57039, Lennox School District 41-4.

## Permits and inspections

- https://dlr.sd.gov/electrical/inspections.aspx — state electrical inspections; Sioux Falls and Rapid City have local inspection departments for work inside those city limits.
- https://www.minnehahacounty.gov/dept/pl/faqs/faqs.php — county building permits cover unincorporated Minnehaha County; people inside a city contact that city.

Left out for every town: neighborhood names, housing age, and house style, unless a source above stated them. None of the sources used here described a housing mix in enough detail to repeat.
