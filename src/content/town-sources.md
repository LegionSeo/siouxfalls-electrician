# Town fact sources

Facts on the town pages come from these pages. Inspector names and personal phone numbers from the state list are not repeated on the site.

## Brandon

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon,_South_Dakota — Minnehaha County, five miles east of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 11,048, incorporated 1973, ZIP 57005, post office since 1878, named from Brandon Township, Big Sioux River and Split Rock Creek, Brandon Valley School District, which schools sit inside Brandon and which sit in eastern Sioux Falls.
- https://cityofbrandon.org/inspections — Planning and Inspection Department, 304 Main Ave., Brandon, SD 57005.

No SDDOT or city page was used for driving directions, so the Brandon page does not add a road route.

## Harrisburg

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrisburg,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, suburb of Sioux Falls, about 2.7 miles from the southern edge of Sioux Falls, 2020 population 6,732, Census Bureau 2024 estimate 10,203, founded 1873, ZIP 57032, Harrisburg School District, Adventure, Freedom, and Liberty elementaries inside Harrisburg city limits, other district schools inside Sioux Falls.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_South_Dakota — county seat is Canton.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf — I-29 Exit 71 (Harrisburg) interchange at 273rd Street; 273rd Street provides access to I-29 from Harrisburg.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/533e33d0/Northern%20Lincoln%20County%20Corridors%20Study_Corridor%20Plan_Final_20240404.pdf — 273rd Street is the main east-west road into Harrisburg; SD Highway 115 runs to 274th Street in Harrisburg.

## Tea

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, suburb of Sioux Falls, laid out in 1894, name chosen for its brevity, 2020 population 5,598, Census Bureau 2024 estimate 7,699, ZIP 57064, Tea Area School District.
- https://www.teaarea.k12.sd.us/page/contact-us — Legacy, Venture, middle school, and high school addresses in Tea; Frontier Elementary is in Sioux Falls.
- https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/results.aspx?districtnumber=41005 — Tea Area 41-5, 131 N Poplar, Tea.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf — I-29 Exit 73 (Tea) interchange at 271st Street.

## Hartford

- https://www.hartfordsd.us/welcome — city site: about 10 miles west of Sioux Falls; West Central schools.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_South_Dakota — Minnehaha County, suburban city northwest of Sioux Falls, named Oaksville in 1878, renamed Hartford in 1881, incorporated 1896, post office since 1880, 2020 population 3,354, ZIP 57033, West Central High School, district also covers Humboldt, the Wall Lake area, and Ellis.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/qrppocbx/final-study-report.pdf — SD Highway 38 runs through Hartford and meets I-90 at Exit 390, just east of Hartford.

## Dell Rapids

- https://dot.sd.gov/media/079ac1ff/DellRapids_MTP_Final.pdf — northern Minnehaha County, about 15 miles north of Sioux Falls on the I-29 corridor, bedroom community of Sioux Falls, Big Sioux River, downtown about three miles from I-29 via SD Highway 115, Old Highway 77 north of the city. The plan’s 2017 population figure is an old count, so it is not used as the current population.
- https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/results.aspx?districtnumber=49003 — Dell Rapids School District 49-3, 1216 N Garfield Ave; elementary, middle school, and high school.
- https://www.dr-k12.org/ — Dell Rapids School District 49-3 lists Dell Rapids Elementary, Dell Rapids Middle School, and Dell Rapids High School.

## Canton

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton,_South_Dakota — county seat of Lincoln County, 18 miles south of Sioux Falls, Big Sioux River across from Beloit, Iowa, 2020 population 3,066, incorporated 1881, ZIP 57013, part of the Sioux Falls metropolitan area, South Dakota Community of the Year Award in 2004 and 2011, Lincoln County Courthouse built in 1889.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_South_Dakota — Canton School District 41-1 is a district in the county.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf — I-29 Exit 62 (Canton) interchange is US Highway 18.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/4f2fbf9f/SDDOT_LincolnCountyMTP_FinalReport_20191118.pdf — US 18 runs from I-29 to Canton.

## Lennox

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lennox,_South_Dakota — Lincoln County, 20 miles southwest of Sioux Falls, settled in 1879, named for Ben Lennox, founded 1889, 2020 population 2,423, ZIP 57039, Lennox School District 41-4, Lennox Elementary, LWC Intermediate, LWC Junior High, and Lennox High School.
- https://www.lennox.k12.sd.us/ — Lennox School District.
- https://doe.sd.gov/ofm/school.aspx?districtnumber=41004&schoolsort=4100402 — Lennox Elementary, district 41-4, 305 W 5th Ave.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/18a17956/17-089ReportFINAL_07.30.18.pdf — SD Highway 44 provides access to I-29 from Lennox; Exit 68 (Lennox / Parker) is at 276th Street.
- https://dot.sd.gov/media/4f2fbf9f/SDDOT_LincolnCountyMTP_FinalReport_20191118.pdf — SD 44 runs from the Turner County line through Lennox to I-29.

## Permits and inspections

- https://dlr.sd.gov/electrical/inspections.aspx — state electrical inspections; Sioux Falls and Rapid City have local inspection departments for work inside those city limits.
- https://www.minnehahacounty.gov/dept/pl/faqs/faqs.php — county building permits cover unincorporated Minnehaha County; people inside a city contact that city.
